About

Engineering context first. Everything here is grounded in real work, real tools and real projects.

I’m a Mechanical Engineering graduate working as a Jr. Design Engineer at CADSYS India Ltd., where my current role focuses on telecom infrastructure design and GIS-driven delivery workflows.

My day-to-day work spans BaseMap utility design, permit exhibits, right-of-way and easement verification, F1/F2 fiber planning, pole identification and validation, and QA/QC before project delivery.

I also build automation projects and technical utilities outside work. I’m especially interested in roles where engineering judgement, software, spatial data and process improvement meet.
