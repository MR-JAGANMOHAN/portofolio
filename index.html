About

I turn engineering requirements and spatial data into clear, build-ready network designs.

My current work focuses on BaseMap utility designs, permit exhibits, ROW/easement verification, GIS QA/QC and F1/F2 fiber network planning. I use tools such as IQGEO, QGIS, ARAMIS, WALDO, AutoCAD and Katapult PLA to move projects from design inputs to final delivery.

I enjoy structured problem-solving, visualizing complex infrastructure and building repeatable workflows that reduce errors before a project reaches the field.
